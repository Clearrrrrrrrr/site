Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

ClearCaliber Automations ("we," "us") builds done-for-you invoice automation for construction general contractors. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect whatever you enter: your name, company, email address, phone number, and message. That form opens your own email client with the details pre-filled and sends the message directly to our inbox, it does not pass through a server or database we control. If you email us directly, we receive whatever you choose to send.

How we use it

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule a call, and, if you become a customer, to set up and support your automation. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site asks for your consent before loading any analytics. If you accept, we may use a privacy-focused analytics tool to understand which pages are visited and how, in aggregate, no attempt is made to identify you personally. If you decline, no analytics are loaded. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you become a customer, your automation setup involves your own Gmail or Outlook account, your own dashboard, and an AI service used to read and extract invoice data, all governed by those providers' own privacy terms and your existing agreements with them.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information you share with us. Because our contact form sends mail directly rather than storing submissions on a server, there is no customer database on this website to be exposed by a website breach.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to clearcaliberautomation@gmail.com.
