From vendor email to clean record, automatically.

No new inbox, no new software to log into. It works quietly in the background on the tools you already have.

1

Vendor emails the invoice

It arrives in the inbox you already use, Gmail, Outlook, or anything else, with no new address to give out and no portal for vendors to learn.

2

Our AI reads and extracts it

Line items, totals, PO numbers, and job numbers, pulled from scans, multi-page PDFs, credit memos, even invoices in other languages or currencies.

3

Anything unclear gets flagged

An unreadable scan or a missing PO still gets logged right away, just tagged "Needs Review" so a person can take a quick look. Nothing sits stuck in an inbox.

4

Every invoice lands in your dashboard

Organized by job and PO, with the original invoice attached. Confident ones are marked ready to go, flagged ones are tagged for review. Either way, it's in the system.
