The Problem

You didn't get into construction to do data entry.

Most GC offices are running invoice tracking on willpower and a shared inbox. It works, until it doesn't.

Invoices die in your inbox

PDFs, photos of paper invoices, and multi-page packing slips pile up in a shared email inbox until someone finally gets to them, or they get missed entirely.

Someone's retyping everything by hand

Line items, totals, PO numbers, and job numbers all get keyed into a spreadsheet or Airtable one invoice at a time. It's slow, and it's exactly the kind of task where mistakes happen.

Duplicates slip through, job costs get messy

The same invoice gets paid twice because nobody caught it, and when you need to prove costs on a job, the paper trail is scattered across a dozen email threads.

How It Works

From vendor email to clean record, automatically.

No new inbox, no new software to log into. It works quietly in the background on the tools you already have.

Vendor emails the invoice

It arrives in the inbox you already use, Gmail, Outlook, or anything else, with no new address to give out and no portal for vendors to learn.

Claude AI reads and extracts it

Line items, totals, PO numbers, and job numbers, pulled from scans, multi-page PDFs, credit memos, even invoices in other languages or currencies.

Anything unclear gets flagged

An unreadable scan or a missing PO still gets logged right away, just tagged "Needs Review" so a person can take a quick look. Nothing sits stuck in an inbox waiting.

Every invoice lands in your Airtable

Organized by job and PO, with the original invoice attached. The confident ones are marked ready to go, and the flagged ones are tagged for review. Either way, it's in the system, not buried in email.
