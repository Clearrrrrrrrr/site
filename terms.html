What we do

ClearCaliber Automations builds and configures a dedicated, private invoice automation system for each customer, built around that customer's own vendors, email inbox, and accounting software. Every customer gets their own separate instance; we do not operate a shared multi-tenant platform.

The system reads vendor invoices sent to your inbox, extracts their data, and checks each one for problems. Invoices that pass those checks are posted to your accounting software automatically. Invoices with a possible problem are flagged for your team to review and correct before they're posted.
